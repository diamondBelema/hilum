History

Most study tools score you on right and wrong. That misses the useful part: a confident wrong answer is a different problem from a lucky right one. And a tutor that forgets you between sessions, or invents a fact, is worse than a textbook.

Assessment

  • Mastery comes only from recorded evidence. Not from the agent's opinion, and not from you saying "I get it".
  • Confidence and "I don't know" on every question. They change the score and the plan.
  • Every fact cites your notes. A note, a place in it, and a short quote, or it is marked unverified.
  • Goals with time. Set an exam date and Hilum paces the plan and tells you when you are behind.
  • Drop your files in, name your courses. Hilum sorts, converts and maps them to concepts. You approve the plan.

Plan: how it works

1. Drop
Slides, PDFs and phone photos go in one inbox. Hilum files them by course and flags anything unclear for you.
2. Teach
Your own AI agent teaches in a live session: explains, asks, listens to what you think, adjusts.
3. Test
Questions, calculations and teach-back, graded, with evidence stored per concept.
4. Review
Pick concepts on the knowledge graph to practise, or let decaying ones come back on schedule.

Local-first and private

Hilum has no AI inside it. It is a server and a notes folder on your computer. Your agent connects over MCP and does the teaching. Nothing is uploaded to us, because there is no us: no accounts, no analytics, no hosted backend.

Your notes are plain Markdown. If you stop using Hilum, you still have everything. One honest caveat: whichever model you connect receives what it needs to teach you, so choose one you trust. A local model keeps everything on your machine.
