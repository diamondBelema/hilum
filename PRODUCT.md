# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

A medical student studying for hours, often at night, often on a mid-range Android phone, sometimes on a laptop. Primary audience is the student themselves during solo study sessions. Secondary audience is the student during timed exam practice. They are tired, under time pressure, working in variable lighting (dark rooms at night, bright daylight outdoors), often offline, and need the app to feel calm and legible rather than stimulating.

## Product Purpose

A local-first study and exam-simulation harness for medical students. Core jobs: (1) take a timed exam simulation, (2) read and cite notes, (3) answer activities in a live teaching session, (4) see what to study next, (5) review results and the knowledge graph. Success looks like: the student can open a note and read it in under 100 ms, complete a timed exam with the timer and answers surviving a reload, and see their mastery state for every concept without color being the only signal.

## Positioning

A calm, clinical, local-first study tool built around the mastery ring — a three-arc ring that appears everywhere a concept appears. Everything else stays quiet. No marketing dashboard, no grid of identical rounded cards, no neon accents.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clinical, specific to anatomy and physiology students. Quiet chrome, legible typography, meaning never depends on color alone. H&E histology-inspired palette (hematoxylin blue-violet, eosin pink) as identity, but restrained.

## Anti-references

- Cream paper with a terracotta accent
- Near-black with a neon accent
- Grid-of-identical-rounded-cards dashboard
- Gradients, glows, or colored shadows
- Color as the only signal for state
- Placeholder text used as a label
- Animations that run without a user action

## Design Principles

1. Usability and legibility under fatigue and time pressure.
2. Speed on a low-end Android phone.
3. Consistency.
4. Visual identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text 4.5:1 or better, UI component boundaries 3:1 or better, in both light and Black (default dark) themes. Full keyboard operation for every flow. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-color-scheme`. Works at 200% zoom and at 360px width. Touch targets at least 44x44 px. Nothing below 13px. Never remove focus outlines.
