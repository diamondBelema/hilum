# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web, local-first. Runs on the student's machine and is used from a laptop, a
tablet or a mid-range Android phone.

## Users

A medical student studying for hours, often at night, often on a phone,
sometimes on a laptop. They are tired, under time pressure and often offline.
They need calm, legible screens, not stimulation.

## Product purpose

Hilum is a local-first study and teaching harness built around the live
session. The student says what they want to learn, an agent teaches through
short prose turns and small activities, and the whole session is saved and
replayable. Notes, exams, questions and the knowledge graph feed that loop.
They are first-party, but quiet.

Success looks like:

1. The student types a goal and a session starts.
2. The agent explains, asks, listens and adapts; the transcript is readable
   without a manual reload.
3. Answers are saved and shown read-only, and a reload restores the session.
4. Mastery comes from recorded evidence, and citations lead back to the vault.
5. The student can open the Library for notes, exams, questions, progress and
   the graph without those surfaces competing with sessions.
6. Everything works at 360px, offline, in both themes and with a keyboard.

## Core jobs, in order

1. Learn in a live teaching session with an agent.
2. Practise banked questions; take a timed paper when the student chooses to.
3. Read and cite notes, and follow a citation from the transcript.
4. See what to study next and what is due for review.
5. Review results and the knowledge graph.

## Product principles

1. **The session is the product.** Notes, exams, questions, progress and the
   graph exist to feed it or to be looked at from time to time.
2. **The agent is a teacher, not a form engine.** It explains, asks, listens
   and adapts. Hard rules are few.
3. **Everything in a session is saved and replayable.** Same components, same
   answers, same order.
4. **Secondary surfaces are first-party but quiet.** One Library entry, never
   the home screen.
5. **Local-first and calm.** Legible under fatigue, at 360px, offline, in both
   themes.
6. **Mastery comes from recorded evidence**, and citations link back to the
   vault.

## Positioning

A calm, clinical, local-first study tool built around the mastery ring and the
live session. No marketing dashboard, no grid of identical rounded cards, no
neon accents.

## Brand personality

Calm, clinical, specific to anatomy and physiology students. Quiet chrome,
legible typography, meaning never depends on color alone. H&E
histology-inspired palette (hematoxylin blue-violet, eosin pink), restrained.

## Anti-references

- Cream paper with a terracotta accent
- Near-black with a neon accent
- Grid-of-identical-rounded-cards dashboard
- Gradients, glows, colored shadows or backdrop blur
- Color as the only signal for state
- Placeholder text used as a label
- Animations that run without a user action

## Design principles

1. Usability and legibility under fatigue and time pressure.
2. Speed on a low-end Android phone.
3. Consistency.
4. Visual identity.

## Accessibility and inclusion

WCAG AA: body text 4.5:1 or better, UI component boundaries 3:1 or better, in
both light and dark themes. Full keyboard operation for every flow, including
the mobile drawer and the course picker. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and
`prefers-color-scheme`. Works at 200% zoom and at 360px width. Touch targets at
least 44 by 44px. Nothing below 13px. Focus outlines are never removed.
